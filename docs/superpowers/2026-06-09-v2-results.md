## Two important findings (both counter-intuitive — that's why we measured)

1. **Small segments (40–200 words) made it WORSE**, not better. They introduced **12–25 errors** because a tiny window doesn't give GPT enough context, so it "fixes" valid words (`ובניטרולה`, `הפלסטיק`). **400-word chunks (more context) are clearly best.** Your "scan 40 words" hypothesis was worth testing, but the data rejected it.
2. **The entity generator only works "knowledge-first."** v1 of it echoed the *garbled* names (`תוהר`, `אמפתי LV`) — useless. Telling it "use your knowledge for the spelling, the transcript only for *which* entities" flipped it to correct spellings (`ToHa`, `אמפא TLV`).

## The remaining gap (46 → 31, want → single digits)

The ~31 remaining are:
- **Niche private names GPT doesn't know** (`בית אמצור`, `סרוגו`, `עזריאלי טאון`, `אמפא TLV`) — GPT omits them (correctly, rather than guess). **These need the company's published report** (MAYA/IR fetch) — the next V2 step.
- **Domain terms** (`שיעור היוון`) and **numbers** — correctly *flagged* for the analyst, not auto-fixed (by design).
